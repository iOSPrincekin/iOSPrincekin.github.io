About This Long Mission

Why Mars?

Mars represents a simple engineering spirit: acknowledge the unknown, respect the constraints, and turn the impossible into testable problems one step at a time.

This site documents my software development expeditions. It focuses on real engineering problems: mobile development, system design, toolchains, performance, reliability, and the details that reshape how I think about building software well.

Writing Principles

  • Start with the problem, its constraints, and the reasoning behind each decision.
  • Provide reproducible code, data, or verification methods.
  • Document failed paths; they are often more valuable than the final answer.
  • Respect the reader’s time and keep complex ideas clear.
